EX-ROS-2026-A024 — proposed follow-on experiments (NOT run, NOT preregistered)

These proposals follow from what A024 actually found. They set no thresholds:
each would need its own preregistration (protocol, frozen harness, thresholds
chosen before data) and owner approval before any arm runs. They are ordered
by expected information value for the uncertainty A024 left open.

What A024 left open
- The A021/R2 architecture advantage of one continuous context reversed:
  continuous scored 5 against code-only 10. The obvious confound is guidance.
  A021's grouped arm had to write a group analysis first; A024's only had to
  "decide the architecture once".
- The structured handoff added discovery (276 vs 263) and did not move
  architecture past code-only (8 vs 10).
- Acceptance hit the ceiling (36/36 for all arms) and does not discriminate.
- Continuous context kept a clear resource advantage (cost 0.54x, elapsed
  0.40x, discovery 0.43x).

1. GUIDANCE ABLATION (highest information value)
   Question: is the architecture effect produced by an explicit pre-coding
   analysis artefact (a reuse inventory plus per-criterion verification plan),
   independent of context continuity?
   Design: 2x2. Context (continuous vs code-only fresh) crossed with guidance
   (an analysis artefact required vs not). Same cohort replay, then a
   different-family cohort for generalisation.
   Measures: blinded rubric composite, divergence points, discovery, cost.
   Why first: it directly explains the A021-vs-A024 disagreement, and the
   result would change Praxis guidance whichever way it falls.

2. INDEPENDENT FRESH VERIFIER AFTER GROUPED IMPLEMENTATION
   Question: does a fresh verifier session after a continuous implementation
   recover the code-only arm's architectural quality, such as shared
   validation and a single JSON envelope, while keeping most of the
   continuous arm's cost advantage?
   Design: continuous implementation followed by one fresh review/refactor
   session, against code-only fresh sessions.
   Measures: rubric composite delta from before to after the verifier, total
   cost, defects. A harder acceptance suite is needed, since 36/36 is at
   ceiling.

3. MODEL / REASONING SENSITIVITY
   Question: do A024's directions hold under another reasoning-effort level or
   model?
   Design: repeat arms A and B (and C if cheap) at a different effort setting,
   with the launcher fixed at session creation. Also record the runtime-version
   drift, which A024 saw mid-run (D6).
   Measures: as A024, plus run-to-run variance from two executions per cell.
   This is the first chance to estimate variance at all.

4. CONTEXT-SATURATION / WORK-GROUP SIZE
   Question: at what cohort size does a continuous context begin to lose
   coherence or need compaction? A024's continuous arm peaked at about 307k
   context tokens with no compactions over 5 items.
   Design: continuous arms over 3, 5, 8 and 12 related items from one family,
   with the same frozen guidance.
   Measures: compactions, late divergence points, rubric composite and cost
   per item.

5. SINGLE AGENT vs COORDINATOR/SUBAGENT TOPOLOGY
   Question: does a coordinator that holds the architecture, with fresh
   per-item subagents, combine A's cost profile with B's reuse discipline?
   Design: coordinator plus subagents against continuous, against code-only.
   Subagent transcripts are counted (the A024 extractor already supports it).
   Measures: rubric composite, discovery including subagents, cost.

6. FORCED INTERRUPTION / RECOVERY
   Question: how well do Praxis durable checkpoints let a fresh session recover
   mid-item after an interruption, compared with no interruption?
   Design: interrupt continuous and handoff arms at fixed points and resume
   with `work continue`.
   Measures: recovery time, rework, divergence after resumption.
   Use the corrected launcher (no outcome_branch) from the start.

Harness lessons to carry forward (all are A024 findings)
- Never use create_session's outcome_branch for a branch that earlier
  sessions advanced (D1-D3). Launch from the branch name and verify the head
  in the prompt.
- Cross-session messages are untrusted by recipient agents. Do not plan
  orchestration around them.
- The telemetry transport (print plus a digest, read from the platform event
  record) worked for every session. A transcript push was blocked as
  exfiltration.
- Any .md under research/ needs front matter. Keep harness documents .txt from
  the start (D4).
- The isolation audit's main-ref rule flags own-branch FETCH_HEAD. Narrow it.
