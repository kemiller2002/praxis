PRAXIS-GROUP-01: praxis work group create: durable human-declared execution group
Record a declared group (ID, members, kind, execution repository, cross-repository flag, shared context) in Praxis state. Acceptance: refuses unknown or terminal members and duplicate IDs; never changes a member's lifecycle state; the planner reads the stored declaration exactly as it reads grouping.groups; --dry-run and --json supported; validate checks stored groups. Phase two of requirements/PLANNING-WORK-GROUPS.md (covers PRX-GRP-073); proposed cohort of EX-ROS-2026-A021. Must not be merged before that experiment is evaluated and the owner accepts phase two.

PRAXIS-GROUP-02: praxis work group show: a declared group with member states and progress
Read-only view of one declared group: members with their own recorded and planning states, partial-completion progress, blocked members and who they gate, execution repository, architecture notes. Acceptance: text and --json; unknown group exits 1; never writes. Depends on: PRAXIS-GROUP-01. Phase two of requirements/PLANNING-WORK-GROUPS.md (covers PRX-GRP-073); proposed cohort of EX-ROS-2026-A021. Must not be merged before that experiment is evaluated and the owner accepts phase two.
