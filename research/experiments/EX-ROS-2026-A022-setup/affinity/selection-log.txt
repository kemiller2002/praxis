2. Rule-driven change
---------------------
- PRAXIS-CTL-01 removed from the high cohort: it fails H1. Its only couplings
  with CTL-02/03/04 are weak ones (shared host files, surfaces, the JSON
  error body); its diagnostics endpoint could share an envelope with the read
  API, but its acceptance criteria do not require that ('possible'
  relations, not counted). It remains a ready backlog item outside the
  experiment.
- PRAXIS-TELEMETRY-CLASSIFY-VOCAB removed from the low cohort for size
  parity (model.txt section 6): fewest acceptance criteria (4, tied with
  ACTOR-KIND-ANCHOR), then fewest expected files (3 against 19). Its
  affinity counts were not used. It remains a ready backlog item outside the
  experiment.

4. After freezing
-----------------
Nothing may be added, removed or substituted. If an item proves impossible or
invalid during execution, the condition records it (the item is blocked with
its reason and reported), and the analysis reports the cohort with that item
missing in every condition that could not complete it. It is never silently
replaced.
