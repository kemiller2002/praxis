# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

A preregistered comparison reviewed systems engineering, method engineering,
requirements engineering, knowledge and ontology engineering, decision
analysis, cybernetics, quality engineering, organizational learning and
knowledge management, agent orchestration, and design science across 12
frozen dimensions.

No individual field fully covered the exact FE object-and-integration
boundary. Collectively, the adjacent fields substantially covered every
identified FE mechanism. No clearly non-subsumed causal mechanism was found.

## Exact claim supported or contradicted

Supports narrowing FE to a candidate integrated engineering profile and
contradicts any unqualified current claim that FE is a distinct discipline
with a demonstrated unique mechanism.

This is a proposed synthesis finding. It does not overwrite the controlling
`EX-FE-0002` experimental state, which remains inconclusive.

It is counterevidence, but not a complete falsification, of
`HY-FE-2026-B006`: an integration can still create value even if its parts are
inherited.

## Source provenance

Thirty primary, official, or explicitly labeled supporting sources are
registered in the Framework Engineering successor package. The evidence
includes ISO/IEC/IEEE, W3C, OMG, ABET, INCOSE, NIST, NASEM, NASA, FIPA,
INFORMS, professional bodies, and original peer-reviewed method and design
science work. Abstract-only and secondary access is labeled.

## Relevant excerpt or data

The mechanism table maps:

- lifecycle and integration to systems engineering;
- construction and tailoring to method engineering;
- semantics and provenance to ontology engineering and W3C PROV;
- requirements and traceability to requirements engineering;
- uncertainty and tradeoffs to decision analysis;
- measurement and improvement to quality engineering;
- feedback and adaptation to cybernetics and organizational learning;
- provider identity, lifecycle, roles, and handoff to agent engineering;
- artifact build/evaluate logic to design science.

## Interpretation

The residual FE boundary is the selected unit of analysis and integrated
profile, not a source-supported unique causal mechanism. The burden of proof
therefore becomes incremental outcome value over matched established
practice.

## Limitations

This is a broad structured review, not a formal systematic bibliometric
review. Several standards were accessible only as official abstracts.
Comparator fields are internally diverse. Literature cannot establish FE
efficacy. One provider performed the synthesis; it is not the independent
blinded mechanism mapping required by `EX-FE-0002`.

## Counterevidence

Systems engineering, method engineering, and design science each leave a
meaningful residual in either object scope or integration. Therefore
individual full subsumption was not established.
