# Hypothesis

## Statement

For bounded analytical-framework tasks, the full FE integration improves
downstream correct action and evidence integrity over a feature-, effort-,
and documentation-matched composition of established adjacent practices
without unacceptable lifecycle cost.

## Mechanism

The proposed active mechanism is not novelty of the individual practices. It
is their integration around one versioned object model: explicit separation
of purpose/identity, capabilities, semantics, evidence, contradictions,
confidence, context, provenance, execution, and change. This may reduce
semantic loss and recovery time across human and machine handoffs.

## Predictions

- Blind downstream users make more correct application, adaptation, and
  diagnostic decisions from full FE artifacts.
- Unsupported or contradictory claims are found with higher recall and
  acceptable false positives.
- Independent reconstruction and interrupted-work recovery improve.
- Improvements survive documentation-dose, presentation, provider, and task
  controls.
- Lifecycle quality gains are not erased by authoring, review, training,
  compute, cognitive, or maintenance cost.

## Evidence that would support it

- A preregistered full-FE versus strong matched-adjacent comparison exceeds
  minimum practical effects on downstream correct action and evidence
  integrity.
- Full FE outperforms a layout- and effort-matched FE ablation.
- Results reproduce on held-out tasks and independent providers or
  organizations.

## Evidence that would contradict it

- Preregistered equivalence with the matched adjacent profile.
- Benefits disappear when documentation dose and presentation are matched.
- The candidate-specific ablation is equivalent to full FE.
- Cost or cognitive burden exceeds the accepted boundary.
- Provider familiarity or task family explains the effect.
- Independent replication fails.

## Tests performed

No discriminating test has been performed. Existing FE studies exercise
representation and workflow behavior but do not supply the matched causal
contrast.

## Results

Untested. Internal executability is supporting feasibility evidence only.
Adjacent-field overlap is material counterevidence to any uniqueness-based
mechanism.

## Falsification attempts

The 2026-07-28 boundary mission searched for individual and collective
subsumption. Individual full subsumption was not established; collective
functional subsumption was substantially supported.

## Current assessment

Very Low confidence. The integrated profile is coherent enough to test, but
no outcome value has been demonstrated.
