# Context

`FE-MISSION-001` tested whether Framework Engineering has a defensible
non-redundant boundary. The comparison found a coherent object-level
integration, no clearly non-subsumed causal mechanism, substantial collective
coverage from adjacent fields, no matched outcome study, and no independent
institutional practice.

# Decision

Pending governance acceptance:

1. Describe FE as a repository-centered research program and candidate
   integrated engineering profile.
2. Do not describe FE as an established or validated distinct engineering
   discipline.
3. Preserve ROS–FE Profile v1.0 as the accepted-for-pilot baseline.
4. Route enforcement improvements through a versioned v1.1 proposal.
5. Make a preregistered matched incremental-utility experiment the next
   evidence gate.
6. Delay general automatic framework generation, autonomous canonical
   promotion, organization-wide mandates, credentialing, and discipline
   branding.

# Alternatives

- **Declare a discipline:** rejected because distinct mechanisms, independent
  outcomes, external practice, and institutions are absent.
- **Terminate FE:** rejected because no single field fully subsumes the exact
  object and an integrated profile may still have testable value.
- **Build more infrastructure first:** rejected because it does not resolve
  incremental utility and increases sunk cost.

# Consequences

Engineering can consume the proposed definitions, schemas, risks, and test
design as provisional inputs, but not as an accepted efficacy claim. Null and
negative experimental outcomes must trigger simplification or renaming.
Version 1.0 remains reproducible while v1.1 integrity controls are proposed.

# Reversibility and validation

Supersede this decision after:

- controlled and independently replicated comparative evidence establishes or
  falsifies incremental value; or
- a stronger systematic review establishes individual full subsumption.

Validate the decision against `EV-FE-2026-B003` through `B005`,
`HY-FE-2026-B006`, and the Framework Engineering successor REP.
