# Evidence Record

## Evidence summary

Framework Engineering has an executable internal research program: governing
records, provisional theory, hypotheses, experiment records, comparison
instruments, evidence workflows, an accepted-for-pilot ROS profile, provider
execution boundaries, registries, and tests. Current authority nevertheless
describes the field as provisional and retains low confidence in
distinctiveness and efficacy.

`EX-FE-0002` contains a rigorous blinded mechanism-boundary protocol, but its
controlling v1.1 record remains Stage A incomplete, Stage B blocked, and
classification inconclusive. Its machine-only v1.2 pilot is incomplete.
`EX-FE-0003` records no execution data.

## Exact claim supported or contradicted

Supports the claim that FE is currently a repository-centered research
program capable of executing structured investigations. It does not support
the claim that FE is a validated or distinct engineering discipline.

## Interpretation

Repository coherence and executable tooling clear a research-program
threshold. They do not clear a causal, external-validity, professional, or
institutional discipline threshold.

## Limitations

This evidence is internal to one repository and authoring lineage.
Tool-conformance results do not establish framework utility.
The 2026-07-28 source synthesis does not satisfy the prior experiment's
independent audit, blinding, recognition, key-custody, or reviewer gates.

## Counterevidence

The repository has substantial breadth, explicit falsification language, and
multiple provider executions, which make it more than a purely conceptual
proposal. Those facts strengthen the research-program classification but do
not independently establish generality.
